# Reserve 1.12.3

If you use Reserve 0.9.1 or earlier, download Reserve.dmg, open it, and drag Reserve into Applications. Choose Replace, then reopen Reserve.

Reserve could refuse another Claude account because it mistook two members of the same team for the same person. Each member can now have a separate card showing their own usage limits. If you sign in twice with the same member, Reserve tells you which card already uses that account.

After updating, let Reserve refresh. If the card shows your limits, you're done. If it still asks you to sign in, open **Settings → Providers**, click **Sign in** on that card, and choose the team member you want on Claude's sign-in page. You can keep the cards you already connected.
