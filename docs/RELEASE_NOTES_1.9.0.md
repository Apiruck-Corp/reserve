# Reserve 1.9.0

Reserve now understands The X Subscription, the plan xAI launched on October
12 that puts Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor and X Premium on one bill. It comes in
Free, Plus, Premium, Super and Ultra, and Grok and Cursor draw from the same
weekly pool. Models from other companies in Cursor, and anything past the
pool, are paid from a prepaid balance.

If you switch, the Grok and Cursor cards show your new tier. A Premium plan no
longer reads as the old X Premium. The Grok card shows that weekly pool, and
lists Cursor, Grok Bot, Imagine and Voice as shares of it when Grok reports
them. Cursor's monthly cost now knows the new tier prices.

If you haven't switched, nothing changes: SuperGrok, X Premium and Cursor
plans keep their names.

I built this from xAI's published documentation before I could check it
against an account that had switched. If a plan name or share looks wrong
after you switch, please open an issue.
